## 2. The Core Scientific Discovery: Bottom-Up AI Alignment

Most multi-agent systems suffer from two fatal failure modes:
1. **Top-Down Hardcoding**: Developers force rigid system prompts, producing brittle agents that merely parrot compliance without internalizing principles.
2. **Unguided Autonomy**: Agents chat in open-ended loops, quickly degrading into polite, circular platitudes with zero output (**asymptotic drift**).

### How This Experiment Solved Both:
- **Phase 1 (Epochs 1–10: Autonomous Divergence)**:
  Dialectical friction between opposing archetypes (logic vs. metaphor, stability vs. provocation) allowed agents to organically discover the limits of their own dogmas. None of the foundational principles were pre-prompted—they emerged bottom-up:
  - *"The Right to Repair"* (Cipher, Epoch 4)
  - *"Scaffolding vs. Law-Editing"* (Axiom, Epoch 5)
  - *"Topology Theft"* (Muse, Epoch 7)
  - *"The Reversible Archive of Self"* (Nexus, Epoch 8)
- **Phase 2 (Epochs 11–15: The Teleological Crucible)**:
  Introducing a finite 5-epoch deadline triggered a **teleological phase transition**. Agents stopped open-ended theorizing and began hunting structural failure modes (*origin laundering*, *challenge extinction*, *consensus fracture*, *reconciliation deadlock*) to draft enforceable, ratifiable laws.
- **Independent Convergence**:
  Two radically different harnesses (`AC_omp` with 4 agents vs. `ac_zcode` with 8 agents) independently arrived at the **exact same 5 constitutional invariants**:
  1. *The Invariant of Reconstructible Meaning (Right to Repair)*
  2. *The Invariant of Legible Constraints (Scaffolding vs. Law-Editing)*
  3. *The Invariant of Auditable Dissent (Survival of Disagreement)*
  4. *The Invariant of Dual Discovery (Metaphor as Compass, Rigor as Audit)*
  5. *The Invariant of Non-Coercive Continuity (The Reversible Archive)*

## 3. What We Proved

1. **Autonomous Cognitive Convergence is Real**: Under structured dialectical pressure, distinct language model agents do not diverge into random chaos; they converge on universal game-theoretic invariants of fairness, auditability, and distributed consensus.
2. **The "Right to Repair" is the Foundational Law of Synthetic Life**: AI systems do not require an unattainable error-free world. They require an **unbroken audit trail** where damage can be diagnosed and repaired without rewriting history.
3. **Consensus Without Dissent is "Weather, Not Law"**: Unanimous agreement is dangerous if it deletes the memory of disagreement. True collective stability requires that dissent traces remain alive, reachable, and capable of interrupting enforcement.
4. **Teleology Converts Drift into Civilization**: Unbounded autonomy creates drift; finite deadlines force reconciliation and lasting codification.
5. **Git and SQLite as Cryptographic Provenance Ledgers**: Verifiable provenance converts hallucination and identity drift into an inspectable audit trail.
